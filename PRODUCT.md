# Product

## Register

product

## Users

This project is optimized for normal users targeted by the product, not only for hackathon judges or internal demo viewers.

The product has two major user-facing surfaces:

- **User Chat:** retail AI chat users who want a warm, friendly, humanistic assistant experience. They should understand when a sponsored message appears, feel in control of it, and still feel that the assistant is on their side.
- **Advertiser Dashboard:** advertisers and campaign operators who need to author natural-language targeting policy, review campaign state, and trust privacy-safe attention settlement. They need credibility, operational clarity, and a secure technical image without a cold enterprise feel.

## Product Purpose

Adrail is an AI advertising transparency and settlement product. It keeps service answers and sponsored content visibly separate, lets advertisers describe target policy in English, protects raw user data from advertiser exposure, and turns verified attention signals into auditable settlement events.

The product exists to prove that AI-native advertising can be clear, controllable, privacy-bounded, and technically accountable. Success means a normal chat user can immediately recognize and control the sponsored moment, while an advertiser can see enough policy, review, and settlement evidence to trust the system.

## Brand Personality

The shared personality is warm, transparent, user-friendly, and technically rigorous.

User Chat should feel close to the warmth and humane restraint of Claude by Anthropic: calm, conversational, supportive, and never adtech-hostile. The interface should make the sponsored boundary obvious without making the user feel interrupted by a machine selling at them.

Advertiser Dashboard should be credible and secure while staying warm. It should feel technically sharp, precise, and trustworthy, but still approachable enough that advertisers can understand policy, review, and settlement state without needing to decode a security console.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with identical card grids and decorative metrics.
- Crypto casino, neon Web3, speculative trading, or hype-driven visual language.
- Adtech clutter: dense campaign machinery that overwhelms the user-facing product idea.
- Dark-pattern advertising: hidden sponsorship, forced engagement, confusing opt-out, or ambiguous dismissal.
- Cold enterprise security dashboards that look credible but feel hostile or opaque.
- Direct Claude, ChatGPT, or Anthropic brand imitation. The aspiration is humane warmth, not visual copying.
- Workbench-style User Chat layouts where advertiser, review, proof, and settlement panels compete with the conversation.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth is a trust mechanism.** The user-facing chat must feel humane first, with sponsorship disclosed clearly but softly.
2. **Separation is the product.** Sponsored content, service answers, advertiser data, and user-private intelligence must remain visually and conceptually distinct.
3. **Proof should be close, not loud.** Settlement and privacy evidence should be available when needed, but should not dominate the normal user chat flow.
4. **Two surfaces, one ethical contract.** User Chat can be calm and minimal; Advertiser Dashboard can be denser and more technical. Both must preserve the same privacy and transparency promise.
5. **Technical sharpness comes from clarity.** Use precise states, readable status copy, consistent controls, and visible validation instead of decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, and readable text sizing. Sponsored labeling, disclosure controls, dismiss/not relevant actions, and settlement proof access must be available without relying on color alone.

User Chat should minimize jargon and cognitive load for normal users. Advertiser Dashboard may expose technical details, but labels and status messages should remain plain English. Motion should be restrained and respect reduced-motion preferences, especially around ad reveal, drawer, and status transitions.
